Instructions: Use your mouse to click on the chick you want to move. You need to get the chicks to cross the bridges and stand next to 'their' feeder. Quite why a chick can only eat from a coloured tray the same colour as itself and ignores all other food is a mystery, but it's a fun and challenging game nonetheless!
