Instructions: Tetris is a truly classic puzzle game, but to play well you need a quick mind too. Use the arrow keys to move and rotate the falling blocks. The aim of the game is to create full horizontal rows - the more you complete at once, the higher your score. Get four rows at once and you'll be a Tetris star! Press P at any time to pause the game.
